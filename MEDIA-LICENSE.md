## Banner image (`public/img/hero-*.webp`, `hero-*.jpg`)

A composite of two separately licensed works, modified: both were cropped
and resized, and the photograph is masked over the stripes using its own
luminance so that the stripes remain at full colour fidelity.

**Warming stripes** — © Ed Hawkins, University of Reading.
Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GLOBAL-STRIPES-1850-2025-hires.png>

Each stripe is one year of global mean temperature, blue (cooler) at 1850
through red (warmer) at 2025.

**Photograph** — NASA/JPL/USGS, PIA00342. NASA media is generally free to
use; NASA does not endorse this site.
<https://images.nasa.gov/details/PIA00342>
