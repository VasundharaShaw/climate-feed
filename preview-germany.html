What is covered

Umweltbundesamt, BMUV, the Sachverständigenrat für Umweltfragen, and institutes including Agora Energiewende, MCC Berlin, Öko-Institut, DIW, the Wuppertal Institut and PIK. Plus Clean Energy Wire and Klimareporter° for the process itself.

Everything here publishes openly. Nothing is scraped from behind a paywall.

What is missing

There is no single database of German climate policy documents, which is the gap this page is trying to fill. Bundestag proceedings, Länder-level policy, and the paywalled professional briefings are all absent.

If you know a source that belongs here, that is the most useful thing you can send.
